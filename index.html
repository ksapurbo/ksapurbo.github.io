About Me

I'm an Economics graduate and Research Analyst at the Bangladesh Institute of Development Studies (BIDS), working on the labour market and development research. I'm a curious, data-driven early-career researcher.

The researcher in me is curious about how different policies shape our everyday lives, while the data analyst in me uses my skills in Stata, R, and Python to assess evidence and uncover patterns that policies leave behind. Off-screen, you'll find me chasing light and stories through my lens.
